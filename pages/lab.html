STATION 02 // COMPUTE-IN-MEMORY (CIM)

4×4 Memristor Crossbar Array & NeuroSim Model

Analog in-memory Vector-Matrix Multiplication ($I_j = \sum V_i \cdot G_{ij}$) modeled on 3-bit quantized RRAM conductance states ($G_0 \dots G_7$) with simulated column 5-bit ADCs and energy profiling based on research with Dr. Vahdat.

EDUCATIONAL 4×4 DEMO VS. 128×128 NEUROSIM RESEARCH CONFIGURATION: 4×4 Educational Crossbar: An interactive workbench to demonstrate analog Vector-Matrix Multiplication ($I_j = \sum V_i \cdot G_{ij}$) and 3-bit discrete conductance states ($G_0 \dots G_7$).
128×128 NeuroSim Subarray: The research configuration modeled under Dr. Shaghayegh Vahdat for VGG8 on CIFAR-10, utilizing 5-bit column successive-approximation ADCs. All displayed metrics are simulation estimates from the C++ NeuroSim framework, not physical silicon measurements.

STATION 03 // PHYSICAL DESIGN & ASIC FLOW

Static Timing Analysis (STA) & Clock Skew Closure

Interactive register-to-register timing verification modeled on Synopsys Design Compiler and PrimeTime flows. Test how clock frequency, combinational logic delay, and clock skew impact Setup & Hold Timing Slack.

EDUCATIONAL STA SIMULATOR DISCLAIMER: This educational calculator implements standard textbook static timing equations ($T_{arrival} = T_{cq} + T_{comb}$, $T_{required} = T_{clk} - T_{setup} + T_{skew}$, $Slack_{setup} = T_{required} - T_{arrival}$). Real industrial ASIC signoff (e.g. Synopsys PrimeTime or Cadence Tempus) incorporates multi-corner PVT analysis, On-Chip Variation (OCV/AOCV), transition slew degradation, and interconnect cross-talk noise.
