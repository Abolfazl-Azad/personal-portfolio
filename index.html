INTERACTIVE HARDWARE WORKBENCH

Silicon Lab — Live Architecture & Hardware Simulation

An interactive, in-browser engineering environment featuring cycle-accurate microarchitecture execution, analog crossbar matrix arithmetic, and textbook Static Timing Analysis (STA) closure.

STATION 01 RV32I 5-Stage Pipeline

Cycle-by-cycle execution, EX/MEM forwarding, hazard stalls, and branch flushing.

STATION 02 Memristor CIM Sandbox

4×4 educational crossbar demonstrating analog vector-matrix multiplication & 3-bit conductance mapping.

STATION 03 STA Timing Closure

Interactive setup & hold slack analysis with parameterized datapath delays and clock skew.
